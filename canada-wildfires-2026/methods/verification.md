## Internal consistency checks

- C2 vs C4 vs C30: C2 is forest, mapped, union (20.78 M ha); C4 is all land, official-scaled, union (35.79 M ha); C30 is merchantable volume on the scaled union (1,336 M m3). All three are distinct-area figures and consistent, but on different bases. Label them.
- C3 vs C2: the 2.06% window is a sum of annual shares; the like-for-like 2023 to 2026 figure is 5.97% (sum), about 2.9 times.
- C13 vs C14: lightning fires 2,960 (NFD-comparable, no Parks Canada) vs 3,019 (official, with Parks Canada). Both are right on their own basis.
- C15: before 14.1, after 16.3, matches REBUILD.md and the recomputed ratios (14.13, 16.28).
- C12: the only cause-model number in the draft that was not updated after the rebuild.
- C18 vs health files: values match modelled_acute_deaths_national.csv and losses/human_cost_2023_2026.csv.
- C19 and C20: C19's double count carries into C20 and into losses/results.md (record year and 4-year total).
- C23 vs losses: losses/human_cost_2023_2026.csv has an older uncalibrated 2026 building estimate (146) that conflicts with property's 517.
