**Bottom line from the records.** Spending on wildfire capacity has risen several-fold since 2022 and equipment, training and FireSmart work has been delivered with counts. Most of the new federal money (aircraft, satellite, most of WRFI) lands from 2026 to 2029, and none of the programs report an outcome such as fires held small. Provinces budget a base and pay most firefighting out of contingency and supplementary votes after each season. The records show more capacity being bought; they do not yet show a measured change in outcomes.

## 4. Research and monitoring under way

`research_underway.csv` lists 58 items: 5 programs and 53 active grant or contribution agreements (end date after 2026-10-22, $71.3 M). Largest: Wildfire Resilience Consortium of Canada $11.7 M; Thunderbird Collective $5.8 M; Tsilhqot'in active forest management $5.0 M; Biodiversity Pathways $5.0 M; Ontario MNR innovation $3.5 M; UBC proactive fuel management $3.4 M; NWT fire and climate $2.5 M; Atlantic Living Lab (CIFFC) $2.4 M; FPInnovations post-fire examination $2.3 M; U of T LiDAR fuels $2.2 M; CIHR wildfire emergency response $1.5 M; CSA plume modelling (UBC) and peatland fire monitoring (Carleton). No active NSERC or SSHRC wildfire agreement appears in the proactive disclosure file. CFS operational science (CWFIS, fire weather and smoke forecasts, new national fuel type layer) runs inside BTL12.

## Caveats and unreachable pages

- Public Accounts transfer payments for 2025-26 are not in the held InfoBase file, so "paid" stops at March 2025.
- NAS PDF download was an HTML error page and canada.ca refused curl; the target text came from the canada.ca page via WebFetch.
- NRCan departmental plans index returned a 404 page. CIFFC annual report 2024-25 text is empty.
- Alberta 2026 preparedness release and Manitoba waterbomber purchase release were not reached on government sites; both are excluded.
- Quebec, Yukon and Ontario staffing and zone records were not searched in depth.
- Parks Canada, ISC equipment and WildFireSat spending cannot be separated from wider program totals.
