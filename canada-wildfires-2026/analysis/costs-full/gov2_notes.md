# Government response costs, second round (2023 to 2026)

Checked 2026-10-22 (second pass on the municipal items and the Parks Canada 2024 check the same day). Files: `gov2_costs.csv` (51 rows), `gov2_manifest.csv` (56 sources), scripts `scripts/gov2_build_costs.py`, `scripts/gov2_season2026_fit.py`, `scripts/gov2_manifest.py`, `scripts/gov2_links.py`; downloads in `raw/gov2/`; area data used for estimates in `work/gov2_cwfif_area_by_agency.csv` and `work/gov2_season2026_fit.json`.

Same rules as `gov_notes.md`: official records only (news was used only to find documents, and where the official document could not be opened the figure is not counted); a fiscal year Y to Y+1 is fire season Y; 2025 dollars use the same CPI factors (2023 x1.045033, 2024 x1.020723, 2025 x1.0, 2026 x0.975604).

How to read `gov2_costs.csv`: `low`, `central`, `high` are in 2025 dollars. For rows marked `yes` or `partial` they are the amounts to ADD to the bill in the floor, central and high cases. For the three ISC rows (`no (replacement)`) they are replacement values for the gov_ tier C estimate, not additions. A few `partial` rows have a high value of 0 because the health_ file already holds the same money in a high-case-only row; drop them in the high case.

## 1. New Brunswick (2023-24 to 2025-26, and 2026-27)

**Result.** The first round said no actuals exist. They do: NB Public Accounts Volume 2 prints actuals by program component. Forest Protection (fire, insects and disease): $15,578.9k (2023-24), $19,928.0k (2024-25); main estimate $9,026.0k in 2023-24. For 2025-26 the Volume 2 actual is due in December 2026, so the figure is built from the main estimate ($23,290k) plus the Supplementary Estimates 2025-26 Vol 1, which put all $14,000k into Forest Protection. The Q3 2025-26 report: "Natural Resources is projected to be over budget by $14.5 million mainly due to fire suppression and mobilizations associated with wildfires". The audited 2025-26 Public Accounts Volume 1 (Schedule 22) shows Natural Resources at $175.0M against a $161.7M budget, the increase "related to fire suppression and mobilization activities". 2026-27 estimate: $29,126k.

**Method (estimate).** Value = fire share x Forest Protection component - Fire Recovery Out-of-Province revenue ($3,842.6k, $3,166.9k, $3,800k revised, $3,972k budget). Recoveries are removed because those crews sit in the importing provinces' lines, as was done for Quebec. Fire share: low 0.578 (the province's stated "nearly $9 million spent in the 2023 fire season" over the $15.6M component, so suppression alone, a floor); high 1.0 (whole component); central 0.789 (midpoint). In 2025-26 the $14.0M supplementary is counted in full as fire. Federal spruce budworm money goes to Forest Protection Limited directly (NRCan contribution records) and sits in FPL's third-party activities, outside the component.

**Result in nominal dollars.** 2023-24: 5.2 / 8.4 / 11.7M; 2024-25: 8.4 / 12.6 / 16.8M; 2025-26: 23.7 / 28.6 / 33.5M; 2026-27 budget: 12.9 / 19.0 / 25.2M.

**Parks Canada.**
- 2024 season. A Question Period note for the Secretary of State (Nature), received 2025-09-03, says "Parks Canada estimates that the cost of wildfire suppression and response was approximately $163.2M for the 2024 season". gov_ counts $59.8M (Jasper incident response 2024-25) and lists $72.8M re-entry and initial recovery as reference (econ_ counts $20.2M hazard trees; $29.1M interim housing is not counted anywhere). - Check of the $163.2M against Parks Canada's own reports (asked in the follow-up). The 2024-25 Departmental Results Report has no wildfire cost line. It says "Actual expenses were higher than planned by $188.1 million which is primarily due to the additional costs incurred in response to wildfires, particularly in Jasper National Park" and "The Government of Canada provided over $160 million to address immediate needs in Jasper during this fiscal year". That $160M is the whole federal Jasper package of February 6, 2025 ($160.1M: Parks Canada $133.1M including about $30M interim housing, CMHC Housing Accelerator Fund $9.4M, DFAA advance to Alberta $19.6M), so it mixes recovery and other departments. The Parks backgrounder of the same day splits Parks' $133.1M for the 2024 season: up to $45.2M "Incident Response in Jasper", $80.1M "Recovery and Re-entry" (incl. interim housing, hazard trees, the recovery centre) and $7.7M "Other Fire Pressures" (Wood Buffalo and Kootenay fires). The 2024-25 audited financial statements have no wildfire note; Supplementary Estimates (B) 2024-25 have no Parks wildfire item and (C) 2024-25 could not be opened. Public Accounts do not break Parks spending by event.
- Which to use. Keep the Question Period figure ($163.2M) as Parks' season total for suppression and response: it is Parks' own number, it is the only season-wide cost, and the DRR's $188.1M overrun "primarily due to" wildfire response is the same size. Do not use the DRR's "over $160 million" (different scope). For the bill, keep the JRCC actuals for Jasper ($59.8M response, in gov_; $72.8M re-entry as reference) and add the residual: floor $7.7M (fires outside Jasper, funded and in no bill line), central $30.6M, high $54.1M (nominal).
